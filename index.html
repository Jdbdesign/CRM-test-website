FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Bisto CRM.

How long does it take to set up Bisto?
Most teams are fully set up within an hour. We provide a guided onboarding wizard, pre-built templates for common industries, and dedicated support during your first week to ensure a smooth transition.
Can I import my existing CRM data?
Yes. Bisto supports one-click import from Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and most major CRM platforms. You can also import via CSV. Your data will be migrated cleanly with all contact history intact.
Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?
No contracts. All monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Annual plans can be cancelled within the first 30 days for a full refund. We believe in earning your business every month.
Does Bisto integrate with my email and calendar?
Bisto integrates seamlessly with Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, and Calendly. All emails and meetings are automatically logged to the relevant contact and deal records in real time.
Is my data secure and GDPR compliant?
Absolutely. Bisto is SOC 2 Type II certified and fully GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell your data and you retain full ownership of everything you add to Bisto.
